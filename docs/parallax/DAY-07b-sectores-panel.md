## Verdict in one paragraph

Day 7's mechanics hold (0 sub-10px, 0 clips, 0 console errors, Σ widths === band verified to 0.1 pp on 27/27 columns, plate links never truncate) and no judge disputes the Keep list. What the panel found sits one level up, and three of the four judges found the same things independently: **the pages print numbers that contradict the figure next to them.** "Intensity" is own-spend saturation on the Plate and mean risk on the Register, so the shared `?lens=intensity` sorts the Register's Intensity column visibly out of order; the dossier's "Risk over time" axis reads `0.0% · 0.5% · 1.0%` under a caption that says "Peak 27%"; every trajectory (register glyphs, sparklines, hover card, dossier chart and "Span 2001–2025") hangs on a 2001 point with 1–7 contracts, so four sectors that rise from 2002 print `↓`; the model ladder says "sector-specific" on every dossier while serving the identical global CAL-v8 weights (the DB holds the global row once per `sector_id`); the WHAT lede crowns one category and the plate 120px below annotates another as "highest risk"; the dumbbell's "in any federal category · 14%" is the max over 12 of 71 categories while Waste Management's leader holds 85.8%. Around those: a stat strip that leaves five empty cells on every dossier, "Deviation · OECD" over EU lines, USD in the Spanish hero, a 2025 partial year marked nowhere, a log lane whose origin makes "Other" read as zero, and a set of architecture seams (three width hooks, a `useFontsReady` that answers the wrong question, two query keys per endpoint, five hand-rolled URL writers). On the three open questions the panel converged: keep the Garamond Link but encode the exception in the chip primitive; two-line labels help the categories plate (1 → 5 labelled at 1440) and nothing on WHO; the stat-strip holes are span arithmetic (13 units) and a two-line fix.

## The three questions — panel answer

- **Q1 Link vs chip** — unanimous: the Garamond `<Link>` stays (a chip's sans box, icon slot and hover tint would recast the one quiet column of a Cleveland registry). The architect's point stands: the exception is currently eight hand-typed `` `/sectors/${id}` `` literals with a focus ring that differs from the chip's. Encode it in the primitive as `variant="name"` (a bare Link with the formatted name, canonical href + wayfinding state, inherits the caller's font) so rule 1 is grep-enforceable again. The reader's resting affordance (a hairline underline in the border colour) is tried on the crop and kept only if it does not cheapen the plate. The note copy becomes "click a name".
- **Q2 Two-line labels** — measured three ways (canvas `measureText` in the resolved mono after fonts load): WHO gains nothing (every name is one word; Treasury needs 56+6px at 11px in a 54.5px column); categories at 11px, ≤ 2 lines, +6px slack seat **5 of 15** at 1440 EN (Building Construction, Medications & Pharma, Food & Provisions, Road & Highway, the remainder), 3–4 at 1024, 3 in ES, at +14px plate height offset by a shorter legend. The U+2460 circled glyphs render in the Windows symbol fallback at ~9px optical and ⑦⑧⑨ touch on 14/12/9px columns — replace them with a drawn circle + mono digit on both plates. Ship two-line labels on the categories plate only, as a shared `fitLabel` beside `measureLabel`.
- **Q3 Stat-strip holes** — a defect (the dossier's headline block ends in one cell and 788px of paper). 13 track-units is the cause. Drop the `anchor` on High-risk (a 3-character value that never breaks) and drop the Span cell (the range already prints under "Risk over time", and the 2001 min-year is B3's bug) → 12 units, then explicit tracks that divide 12: 0 holes at 390 / 1024 / 1280 / 1440. Folding `anchor` into the shared `StatStrip` and deleting `DenseReadout` is the follow-up (backlog).
